# Norway Civil War 2030 — Map Design

## Intent

A Hearts of Iron IV total-conversion mod set in 2030. The oil fund and the
oil industry have collapsed, the political parties have radicalised, and
Norway has split into a civil war between many factions. The whole game map
is Norway, scaled up, plus a thin strip of Sweden.

This document covers **only the map**: the geography, provinces, states and
starting ownership. Focus trees, ideologies, events, Sweden's focus tree
and the rest of the mod come later as separate projects. The map comes first
because everything else depends on it.

The person making the mod doesn't write code. They playtest. Claude builds
the files, and the person checks the result in-game.

## Goals

- A working, loadable HOI4 map that shows only Norway and a strip of Sweden.
- Many more states than vanilla Norway. States are made from groups of real
  Norwegian municipalities (kommuner), chosen so that **every faction
  border on the approved starting map falls on a state border**.
- Big cities are states of their own: at least Oslo, Bergen, Trondheim,
  Stavanger, Kristiansand, Tromsø, Drammen, Fredrikstad/Sarpsborg and Bodø
  (exact list is the builder's call).
- Provinces inside states: denser around cities and along the coast and
  fjords, sparser across mountains and the inland north.
- Starting ownership exactly as on the approved faction map
  (`faction_map.png`, generated by `make_map.py`; the municipality-to-faction
  table in that script is the source of truth).
- The game starts in 2030 with these tags on the map.

## Factions (starting owners)

| Faction | Who they are | Starting area |
|---|---|---|
| Government (Ap + H) | the old establishment | Østfold, Akershus, Vestfold, lower Buskerud, Telemark coast, Kongsvinger area |
| Western Forces | government forces cut off in the west | inner Sogn, Voss/Hardanger interior, inner Møre (Stranda, Rauma) |
| Jihadist Nationalist Party | Muslim faction | Oslo city |
| KrF | Christian democracy | Agder, most of Telemark, Sirdal |
| Socialists (Rødt + SV) | | Rogaland + Etne/Ullensvang, **and** a separate area in inner Finnmark (Karasjok, Kautokeino) |
| FrP | right-wing libertarian | Trøndelag, southern Nordland, northern inner Møre |
| Nordic Resistance Movement | neo-Nazi | Møre og Romsdal coast and the Nordfjord/Sunnfjord coast |
| Sametinget | Sami | northern Nordland, Troms, most of Finnmark |
| Neutral Coalition (Sp + MDG) | stays out at first | Innlandet and inner Buskerud, Hardangervidda |
| Askøy–Bømlo Insurgency | anarcho-distributionist | islands and coast around Bergen |
| Free City of Bergen | Venice-style merchant republic | Bergen municipality |
| Chaos anarchists | scattered and violent; little equipment, lots of manpower, defensive guerrilla buffs (buffs come later) | single-municipality pockets: Sør-Varanger, Grane, Meråker, Skaun, Rennebu, Sel, Alvdal, Vestre Slidre, Sigdal, Flesberg, Gulen, Våler (Innlandet) |
| Sweden | fully neutral; focus tree comes later | the Swedish strip |

The chaos anarchists own several pockets that aren't connected to each other.
Socialists also start with two separate areas.

## The Swedish strip

A border band deep enough to take in the nearest Swedish cities and not
much more. Roughly: Kiruna in the north, Östersund in the middle, Karlstad
and the Strömstad/Arvika area in the south (builder's call on the exact edge,
but it stays a thin strip). Sweden owns all of it and has no part in the war
at the start.

## Constraints

- It must load in the current HOI4 version without crashing, and the map
  must not throw errors in the game's error log that break play.
- Everything outside Norway and the Swedish strip is ocean or impassable
  wasteland. No Finland, Russia or Denmark as playable land.
- Geography should be real: real coastline, fjords, mountains, lakes and
  major rivers, taken from public geodata rather than drawn by hand.
- The person can't fix code, so the build must be reproducible: scripts in
  the repo regenerate every map file from source data. A change to faction
  ownership or state grouping should be a data edit plus a re-run.
- Repo: `Documents/GitHub/Hoi4 mod` (GitHub: hoi4mod), currently empty.

## Non-goals (this pass)

- Focus trees, ideologies, events, decisions, characters, flags beyond
  placeholders, Sweden's focus tree, balance.
- Svalbard (not on the faction map; left out).
- Polished 3D art: trees, normal map and terrain textures only need to be
  good enough to look like Norway and not be broken.

## Anti-goals

- Faction borders that cut through the middle of a state.
- A map that looks like vanilla Norway with the rest of the world deleted:
  it must feel zoomed in.
- Hand-placed files that can't be regenerated.

## Decided

- States = groups of municipalities that respect faction borders; big cities
  are their own states.
- Sweden starts neutral and owns the strip.
- Starting ownership is the approved faction map.
- Chaos anarchists are **one country** owning all their scattered pockets.
- Western Forces are a **puppet of the Government** at the start and merge
  into it after the war.
- Target: current HOI4 version, **base game with no DLC**.

## Claude's calls (check these)

- **Stable IDs.** State and province IDs live in a registry file in the repo,
  and re-runs keep existing IDs. Later focus trees and events will refer to
  them by number, so they must never be renumbered.
- **States slightly finer than the factions require.** States follow
  traditional districts (e.g. Hardanger, Gudbrandsdalen, Lofoten) inside
  each faction area, so fronts can move and factions can change later
  without regrouping states.
- **Full replacement of vanilla.** All vanilla world content (countries,
  states, history, strategic regions) is replaced or emptied so nothing
  points at land that no longer exists. Sweden keeps the vanilla `SWE` tag
  but gets new, minimal history.
- **Source data.** Municipality borders are the 2024 kommuner from
  github.com/robhop/fylker-og-kommuner (`Kommuner-M.geojson`), the same file
  that produced the approved map.

## Left to the builder

- Map image size, projection and scale (HOI4 needs its map image sizes to
  follow its own rules).
- Province generation method and density, strategic regions, sea zones,
  terrain types, rivers, railways, supply hubs, ports, victory points,
  state categories, manpower and resources (realistic-ish, based on
  population).
- Country tags, colours and placeholder flags.
- Unit/building positions (generated, then the in-game nudger as needed).
- The source data (e.g. Kartverket/OSM municipality borders, a public
  elevation model).
